# Adivansh — World and Art Direction

## Approved visual identity
**Characters:** original ancient Greek-inspired heroic fantasy styling.  
**World:** fictional mythic realm blending Indian and Chinese environmental influences.  
**Presentation:** premium cinematic 2D side-scrolling art.

The story remains Ramayana-inspired in broad themes, but characters must not be dressed as conventional Ramayana depictions.

## Character styling
Use selected Greek/Hellenistic fantasy cues: draped tunics, layered cloth, asymmetrical cloaks, structured leather, bronze and aged-gold armour details, greaves, bracers, geometric borders, engraved motifs and readable heroic silhouettes. This is fantasy design, not strict historical reconstruction. Create original designs rather than copying famous Greek myth characters.

The hero should feel agile and adventurous. The queen needs her own strong silhouette, practical movement and agency. Avoid cultural stereotypes for allies and antagonists.

## World design
Build a third, fictional mythic realm—not a real-world nation or a socioeconomic “third world” setting.

Indian-inspired vocabulary may include stepwells, carved gateways, temple courtyards, ghats, monsoon forests, banyan roots, patterned textiles and warm mineral pigments.

Chinese-inspired vocabulary may include misty mountain passes, bridges, layered roof silhouettes, courtyards, garden paths, bamboo groves, lanterns, carved screens and flowing water gardens.

Shared fantasy vocabulary may include cliff temples, floating ruins, ancient observatories, celestial mechanisms and bridges spanning ravines. Blend influences through coherent regional histories, climates, materials and construction logic rather than mixing motifs randomly.

## Regions
- **Whispering Forest:** monsoon woodland, giant roots, waterfalls, carved stones, mist and fireflies.
- **Forgotten Temple:** stepped courtyards, stone reliefs, shadow puzzles and water channels.
- **River of Ashes:** gorge, suspended bridges, dark volcanic rock and distant mountain silhouettes.
- **Demon Citadel:** basalt fortress, bronze mechanisms, defensive terraces and ominous light.
- **Final Passage:** celestial threshold where the realm's architectural languages converge.

## Palette and composition
Deep teal shadows, forest green, jade, mineral blue, sandstone, aged bronze and muted gold; ember red sparingly for danger. Use a 16:9 reference composition with responsive mobile crop rules. Preserve readable gameplay silhouettes and do not let VFX obscure hazards or objectives.

## Asset pipeline
Brief and references → concept sheets → cultural/originality/licence review → final layered art → sprite/atlas export → Phaser integration → desktop/mobile gameplay review → provenance register.

AI images are concept references until cleaned, reviewed, integrated and rights-checked. Never present concept art as an in-game screenshot.

## Art approval gate
Consistent proportions and materials, clear silhouettes at gameplay scale, smooth animation, readable backgrounds, purposeful VFX, mobile performance and documented commercial rights.
