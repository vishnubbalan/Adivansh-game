# Character Bible — Initial Cast Framework

> Names, final biographies and designs remain subject to narrative and human approval.

## Design principles
Every major character needs a silhouette, motivation, contradiction, capability, vulnerability, voice and visual motif. Avoid using ethnicity, complexion, body shape or cultural origin as a moral signal. The fictional setting may draw from ancient Indic visual vocabulary while remaining original.

## The Seeker — playable hero
**Role:** young royal warrior searching for the abducted queen.  
**Outer goal:** follow the trail and find her.  
**Inner tension:** urgency can become recklessness; leadership requires listening and restraint.  
**Gameplay identity:** agile melee fighter with room for weapon specialisation.  
**Visual needs:** recognisable silhouette at small scale; practical draped garments, protective wraps/armour and original ornamentation; animation-safe cloth and hair shapes.  
**Voice:** direct, observant, emotionally restrained until stakes demand vulnerability.

## The Queen — active story character
**Role:** a central agent whose knowledge is connected to the realm's ancient power.  
**Agency requirement:** she makes plans, resists, negotiates, discovers and affects the outcome while separated from the hero. She must not exist only as a reward or object to retrieve.  
**Narrative rule:** reveal her decisions through authored scenes, messages, actions and later playable perspective where appropriate.

## The Guardian — Chapter One boss
**Role:** protector of an old route and keeper of a clue.  
**Motivation:** a comprehensible duty, oath, corruption or mistaken belief.  
**Encounter:** telegraphed patterns, clear recovery windows and a readable phase change.  
**Visual:** imposing but animation-readable; avoid generic monster shorthand tied to a real culture or religion.

## The Guide — future ally
**Role:** interprets clues and challenges the hero's assumptions.  
**Function:** delivers information in concise, contextual dialogue; does not become a lore-dump device.

## The Antagonist
**Role:** seeks control over an ancient power.  
**Requirement:** coherent worldview, understandable stakes and meaningful consequences. Do not excuse harm simply because the antagonist has a tragic past.

## Character approval dossier
Before final asset production, create for each character: approved name and pronunciation; age range (if relevant); origin and affiliations; biography; goal; fear; contradiction; relationships; dialogue samples; gameplay role; silhouette sheet; front/side/back turnaround; palette/material callouts; animation list; expression sheet; cultural review; asset provenance and licence status.
