# Blessing and Sacred Weapons System

> Design document. The hero earns sacred powers and weapons by helping people, saving innocents,
> and earning the trust of saints and spirits along the journey. This is the Ramayana gift system —
> Rama received divine weapons from sages who tested his worth first. The same principle applies here.

---

## Design Philosophy

Blessings are **earned, not found.** A chest of gold is a reward. A blessing from a saint who watched the hero choose compassion is something different — it changes who the hero is. The player must feel that the blessing is a consequence of their character, not just their route completion.

The mechanic rule: **every blessing has a deed before it.** The deed is always optional — the hero never has to help. But those who choose to will discover that generosity builds power more reliably than grinding.

**Narrative anchor:** Fits the Ramayana structure directly. Vishwamitra gave Rama divine weapons only after testing his discipline. Shabari's devotion blessed his path. Vibhishana's choice to help came from genuine alignment of values. These moments are the template.

---

## Two Types of Sacred Gifts

### Type 1 — Blessings (Passive Powers)
Permanent passive abilities conferred by saints, spirits, and elders after witnessing or receiving help. These modify how the hero moves, fights, and perceives the world. They cannot be earned by fighting alone.

### Type 2 — Sacred Weapons (Active Items)
Special weapons given as gifts. Each has a primary use and a situational power. Sacred weapons use a separate **spirit energy gauge** (not the main stamina bar) so they're powerful but rationed.

## Season 1 — Forest Blessings and Weapons

### Forest Sage (Chapter 8) — Root Spirit Interaction
**Deed:** Hero approaches the agitated Root Spirit with patience, without weapons drawn, and calms it.  
**Blessing received:** **Spirit Sight** — passive. Can see hidden spirit entities, hidden paths, and Mahaksha's corruption nodes that are otherwise invisible. Essential for finding secret areas.  
**Visual:** Eyes gain a faint golden shimmer briefly when the ability activates

---

### The Old Soldier (Chapter 13) — NPC Ally Quest
**Deed:** Hero brings medicine from Chapter 9's cache to a veteran warrior who is wounded and alone.  
**Blessing received:** **Warrior's Read** — passive. Enemy attack windows (safe attack moments) are highlighted briefly in amber. Helps newer players, rewards veterans who helped without expecting reward.  
**Sacred weapon given:** **The Sharpened Blade** — not a sacred weapon per se, but a weapon upgrade that is the bridge to the first true sacred weapon.

---

### Root God Temple (Chapter 20) — Dungeon Completion
**Deed:** Complete the temple dungeon, free the spirits inside from Mahaksha's bindings.  
**Blessing received:** **Root Endurance** — passive. Health regenerates 1 pip per 90 seconds when standing on natural ground (soil, forest floor, grass). Does not work on stone or metal.  
**Lore note:** The Root God's blessing is the forest itself recognising a defender.

---

### The Freed Forest Guardian (Chapter 22) — Boss Post-Defeat
**Deed:** Defeat the Corrupted Forest Guardian without destroying any of the spirit shrines in the arena (optional constraint — they can be destroyed without penalty but not without consequence).  
**Sacred weapon given:** **The Vanara Bow** — ranged weapon. Fires arrows of forest-spirit light. Can be charged: basic shot = quick interrupt; full charge = pierces armour and corrupted entities. Uses spirit energy. Receives Vahana's Scout data to auto-aim at invisible targets if Spirit Sight is active.  
**If shrines were destroyed:** The Guardian still dies but gives no weapon — only the warning. The bow can be earned in Chapter 23 by backtracking, but a small spirit quest is added.

---

### Season 1 Total Progression (Blessings Path)

| Chapter | Deed | Reward | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | Calm the Root Spirit without violence | Spirit Sight | Passive blessing |
| 13 | Bring medicine to the Old Soldier | Warrior's Read | Passive blessing |
| 19 | Find the Hidden Shrine (optional) | Shrine Blessing (+1 max health or +20% spirit energy) | Optional bonus |
| 20 | Free the temple spirits | Root Endurance | Passive blessing |
| 22 | Defeat Guardian without destroying shrines | The Vanara Bow | Sacred weapon |

A player who helps everyone in Season 1 arrives at Chapter 25 with 4 blessings and 1 sacred weapon. A player who skips all optional deeds arrives with 1 blessing (the bow, earned differently) and 2. The generous path is meaningfully stronger but not required.

## Season 2 — Mountain Blessings and Weapons

### The Stone Oracle (Chapter 29)
**Deed:** Bring the Oracle a lore fragment from the ruins (must have been collecting lore — found in Chapters 9, 19, 20).  
**Blessing received:** **Stone Memory** — passive. When entering an area for the second time (after death or chapter revisit), all enemy positions are briefly visible for 5 seconds. The mountain remembers what happened here.

### The Stoneclan Elder (Chapter 34 — after full alliance)
**Deed:** Complete the full alliance arc without betraying the Elder's secret to anyone.  
**Sacred weapon given:** **The Shila Strike** — a heavy stone gauntlet weapon. Ground slam: massive area, destroys ice barriers. Hold down: creates a stone platform briefly (useful for platforming, also blocks projectiles). Uses spirit energy.

### The Blind Prophet (Chapter 38)
**Deed:** Bring the Prophet the specific story item from Chapter 29 (they are waiting for it).  
**Blessing received:** **Prophet's Ear** — passive. Hear enemies through walls (audio indicator + slight pulse icon shows enemy position through thin walls). The Prophet gives knowledge because he received it from someone who had it.

### The Mountain Hermit (new NPC — Chapter 44, optional)
A hermit meditating at the summit. Only visible if Spirit Sight is active (Season 1 blessing). Has been waiting for someone worth talking to for years.  
**Deed:** Sit with the hermit for 60 seconds (player input: hold the interact button, the timer counts, all enemies leave the area).  
**Blessing received:** **Summit Will** — passive. Stamina depletion rate reduced by 30% at all altitudes. The patience required to earn this blessing is exactly the patience it teaches.

## Season 3 — Ocean Blessings and Weapons

### The Ocean Spirit Covenant (Chapter 61)
**Blessing received (from covenant trials):** **Sea Lungs** — passive. Breath timer extended by 50%. Water movement speed increased by 25%.  
**Sacred weapon given:** **The Varuna Lance** — a thrown water-spear. Pierces through any barrier, including Mahaksha's ward anchors. Limited charges, refilled at water sources. Essential for Chapter 73 (ward destruction).

### The Freed Kraken (Chapter 65)
**Deed:** Free the Kraken without killing any of its tentacles (they are part of the creature, not independent enemies — treat as environmental hazard).  
**Blessing received:** **Depths Calm** — passive. No environmental damage from water current, underwater pressure, or ocean storm. The ocean recognises someone who took care not to harm what didn't need to be harmed.

### An Old Fisherwoman (Chapter 52 — village NPC, optional)
The fisherwoman has lost her boat to Mahaksha's naval patrols. The hero cannot directly get the boat back (it's gone) but can sit and hear her story. That's all.  
**Blessing received:** **River Memory** — passive. All hidden food sources in ocean/coastal chapters are faintly glowing (connects to the Hunger system — she has spent her life knowing where food grows).  
**Design note:** This blessing is about witnessing grief without trying to fix it. She asks for nothing. She gives the blessing because someone stopped and listened.

## Season 4 — Island Blessings and Weapons

### A Captive Sage in Ashura (Chapter 77 — The Slave Pits)
**Deed:** Free the sage without alerting any guards (pure stealth — a test of everything the stealth system taught).  
**Blessing received:** **Brahmastra Fragment** — passive. The first fragment of the Brahmastra knowledge. Increases all sacred weapon damage by 40%. The sage says: *"This is not my knowledge to give — it is the realm's. I am only returning what was always yours."*

### Meera Devi's Garden Inscriptions (Chapter 83 — read all inscriptions)
**Deed:** Read all 7 inscriptions Meera has carved into the garden walls (requires Spirit Sight from Season 1 to see the hidden ones).  
**Blessing received:** **Queen's Guidance** — passive. During any fight, Meera's presence through the walls will briefly highlight the safest direction to move (once per 10 seconds). This is not a tactical assist — it is a story mechanic. She has been watching the fights from her window.

### The Homeward Flame (Chapter 90 — Path of Fire, optional shrine)
Deep in the volcanic path is an untouched shrine where offerings from home were left by Ashura's own prisoners. Something sacred survived Mahaksha's corruption here.  
**Sacred weapon given:** **The Jyoti Blade** — the most powerful weapon in the game. Sacred fire that damages corruption and pure-energy attacks specifically. Only available here, Chapter 90, if Spirit Sight is active and the player has at least 3 other sacred weapons. The blade is the culmination of the journey, not a gift — it recognises the whole path.

## Technical Implementation

**Blessing registry:** `src/game/BlessingRegistry.ts`  
Stores which blessings are active as a simple Set of blessing IDs. Persists in save state.

**Sacred weapon inventory:** `src/game/WeaponInventory.ts`  
Array of equipped sacred weapons (max 4 slots). Spirit energy tracked here.

**Deed tracking:** Quest flags already in `StorySystem`. Add `BlessingDeed` flags alongside quest progression flags.

**Visual feedback:** When a blessing is received — full-screen shimmer effect (via VfxManager), a unique sound from AudioManager, a text card: `[Blessing received: Spirit Sight]`.

**Save state:** BlessingRegistry and WeaponInventory serialise into the same chapter save object. Never reset between sessions.
