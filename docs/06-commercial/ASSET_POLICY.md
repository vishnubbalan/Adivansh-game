# Adivansh — Asset Provenance and Commercial Rights Policy

Every shipped visual, audio, font and code asset must have a traceable source and permitted commercial use. Concept art is not automatically safe to ship.

## Asset register fields
Asset identifier/path; type/use; creator/vendor/tool/source; date; licence/terms version; commercial-use/modification/redistribution permissions; attribution; model/tool restrictions; reviewer; approval status.

## AI-assisted content
AI output may be used for ideation. Before shipping, review quality and artefacts, check tool terms and restrictions, avoid copying protected characters/franchises or imitating living artists, confirm fit with approved art direction, record human review and integration status. Do not claim generated output is automatically copyright-cleared or exclusive.

## Third-party content
Review licences for fonts, sound, music, plugins, libraries, textures and marketplace assets. Keep required notices/attribution. Unknown-rights assets must not ship.

## Cultural care
Research ancient Indic, Indian and Chinese visual references thoughtfully. Avoid sacred symbols or religious figures as generic enemy decoration. Maintain fictional coherence and seek informed review for sensitive references.

## Release gate
No asset with unknown provenance or incompatible licence may enter a commercial release build. Record approved, pending and rejected status.
