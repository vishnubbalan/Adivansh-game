# Adivansh — Commercial and Launch Strategy

## Principle
Validate player value before investing in extensive infrastructure or choosing monetization. Frequent builds and green CI are not evidence of market demand.

## Audience
Target players of premium 2D action-adventures, exploration platformers and story-led fantasy. Secondary audiences include fans of myth-inspired stories and cross-cultural fantasy art. Validate segments through playtests and market research.

## Validation sequence
1. Complete Chapter One as a polished vertical slice.
2. Run moderated playtests with a representative small group.
3. Measure onboarding, completion, friction, enjoyment and interest in continuing.
4. Test an accurate landing-page description and concept imagery.
5. Evaluate distribution and pricing after evidence of interest.
6. Expand content based on feedback and sustainable production capacity.

## Monetization options to evaluate
Premium one-time purchase; free opening chapter with paid full-game unlock; or demo leading to paid release on a suitable platform. No model is approved. Compare platform/payment fees, tax, refunds, regional pricing and support. Avoid ads, subscriptions, pay-to-win, loot boxes and manipulative prompts in the initial experience.

## Distribution
Start with web distribution for rapid testing. Evaluate PC storefronts or mobile stores only after understanding packaging, input, performance, compliance and support.

## Metrics
Establish baselines for page-to-start conversion, onboarding, chapter completion, voluntary return, performance, player satisfaction, accessibility feedback and purchase intent. Collect minimal data, disclose analytics and obtain consent where required. Do not profile minors without specialist review.

## Launch readiness
Stable release and rollback; accurate screenshots/trailer/store description; support channel; privacy notice and service inventory; commercial rights for art/music/fonts/code; pricing/refund/tax/platform requirements reviewed; human release approval.
