# Product and Market Validation Plan

## Purpose
Reduce commercial risk before investing in multiple chapters, expensive art production or online services. A playable prototype is not evidence of product-market fit.

## Hypotheses to test
- Target players understand the premise and want to try the game.
- The art direction is distinctive and appealing at actual gameplay scale.
- Controls and combat feel responsive on desktop and mobile.
- Players can complete Chapter One without coaching.
- The chapter ending creates interest in continuing.
- Some segment may be willing to pay for a complete experience.

Treat each as a hypothesis, not a claim.

## Research sequence
1. Define target player segments and recruitment criteria.
2. Prepare a playable build and short neutral test brief.
3. Observe first-time play without coaching; note confusion and abandonment.
4. Conduct a short interview and structured questionnaire.
5. Separate observed behaviour from stated preference.
6. Prioritise high-frequency/high-impact issues.
7. Retest after fixes.
8. Only then test landing-page positioning, pricing and distribution assumptions.

## Suggested measures
Task completion; time to first meaningful action; control errors; deaths/retries; completion rate; points of confusion; perceived fairness; visual recall; desire to continue; accessibility barriers; purchase intent. Establish sample size and interpretation limits before drawing conclusions.

## Ethics and privacy
Explain the purpose of testing, collect informed consent, minimise data, allow withdrawal and avoid collecting unnecessary identifiers. Do not recruit or profile minors without appropriate guardian, legal and privacy safeguards.

## Go / revise / stop decision
Proceed when core gameplay is understood, completion and satisfaction evidence are encouraging, major accessibility/performance issues are addressed and production costs appear sustainable. Revise when friction is fixable. Stop or materially reposition if repeated tests show weak player value or unsustainable production economics.

## Commercial model
Premium purchase, free opening chapter with paid unlock, or demo-to-purchase are hypotheses only. Compare platform fees, taxes, refunds, customer support, regional pricing and acquisition costs. Do not add monetisation until player value and platform obligations are clear.
