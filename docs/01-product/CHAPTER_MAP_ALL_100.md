## Chapter Design Rules

Every implemented chapter must specify:
1. **Player objective** — one clear, stated goal
2. **Emotional beat** — what should the player feel during and at the end
3. **Mechanic** — which system is in focus (combat, traversal, puzzle, story, hybrid)
4. **New element** — what does this chapter introduce that wasn't in the previous chapter
5. **Completion condition** — the exact flag/event that marks this chapter done
6. **Failure state** — what happens if the player fails and how they recover
7. **Optional content** — any secrets, lore, or bonus encounters

The agent team implements chapters one at a time, starting with the current Chapter 1 completion, then sequentially forward. Never skip chapters. Never mark a chapter complete until a human has played start-to-finish.
