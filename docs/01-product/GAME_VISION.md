# Adivansh — Game Vision and Product Brief

## Product summary
**Genre:** premium 2D side-scrolling action-adventure with exploration, puzzles and light RPG progression.  
**Platforms:** desktop and mobile web browsers initially.  
**Priority:** commercial viability.  
**First milestone:** a complete, polished Chapter One vertical slice.

Adivansh is an original mythic fantasy adventure inspired by the emotional and thematic foundations of the Ramayana: separation, loyalty, courage, duty, friendship, sacrifice and the search for a loved one. Its visual identity is intentionally distinct from conventional Ramayana illustrations.

## Player objective
The player controls a young royal warrior whose queen has been abducted by a powerful force. Follow clues across a dangerous mythic realm, overcome guardians and rival factions, solve ancient mechanisms, gain allies and ultimately find the queen. Discover why she was taken and what ancient power is shaping the conflict.

The queen must be written as a fully realised character with agency, knowledge and choices—not merely a rescue objective.

## Core loop
1. Enter a richly illustrated region and understand the immediate objective.
2. Explore paths, clues, resources and secrets.
3. Fight enemies using readable, responsive combat.
4. Solve environmental puzzles and interact with characters.
5. Earn rewards and unlock meaningful abilities/equipment.
6. Reach a chapter climax and reveal the next story lead.

## Design pillars
- **Cinematic premium 2D:** original art, strong silhouettes, expressive animation, parallax and purposeful camera work.
- **Responsive action:** low-latency controls, readable attack telegraphs and satisfying hit feedback.
- **Purposeful exploration:** secrets and environmental storytelling reward curiosity.
- **Meaningful progression:** new abilities change tactics; avoid grind and number inflation.
- **Emotional narrative:** character motivations and consequences drive the adventure.
- **Accessible reliability:** readable UI, audio settings, keyboard/touch parity and stable performance.

## Intended audience
Primary: players who enjoy premium 2D action-adventures, platform games, exploration, combat and story-led fantasy; teen and adult players; desktop/mobile browser players who value easy access.

Secondary: fans of Indian epic-inspired themes, cross-cultural fantasy worlds, light RPG progression, puzzles and boss encounters.

Exact age rating, regional markets and distribution platforms must be validated before commercial launch. Do not target children or collect minors' data without appropriate legal/privacy review.

## Positioning
Compete through an original cross-cultural mythic setting, memorable art direction, polished combat and a compact finished experience—not by claiming to be an open-world AAA game.

## First-release non-goals
Massive open world, multiplayer, always-online requirements, blockchain/NFT systems, unbounded AI-generated story, five incomplete chapters presented as finished, or monetization before player value is validated.

## Success measures
Establish baselines through playtests: onboarding completion, Chapter One completion, combat fairness, visual memorability, target-device performance, interest in the next chapter and willingness to pay. Do not invent numeric targets before collecting evidence.
